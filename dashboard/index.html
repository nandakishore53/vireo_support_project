Data Quality Register & Operational Limitations

Transparent documentation of the 10 known limitations, mitigations, and residual risks.

ℹ
Click any limitation item below to expand the engineering impact analysis, applied pipeline mitigation, and operational guidance.
1. Legacy Timestamp Reconstruction (+5:30 UTC/IST Offset) ▾
Issue: 2,309 legacy tickets migrated from Freshdesk prior to the 14 September 2025 cutover had resolved_at < created_at due to an uncoordinated platform migration where resolution timestamps were stored in UTC while creation timestamps were in IST (+5:30).
Mitigation: Deterministic 330-minute (+5.5 hour) adjustment in src/metrics_engine.py eliminates 100% of negative durations.
Remaining Risk: Microsecond resolution times are mapped to nearest minute intervals.
2. Voluntary CSAT Response Bias (44.22% Response Rate) ▾
Issue: 5,196 out of 11,750 tickets (44.22%) contain CSAT feedback. Voluntary feedback is bimodally polarized.
Mitigation: Empirical Bayes shrinkage ($M=25$) shrinks low-sample noise toward operational peer group baselines.
Remaining Risk: Sentiment of the 55.78% non-respondents remains unobservable.
3. Premature CSAT on Open / Pending Tickets (249 Tickets) ▾
Issue: Exactly 249 tickets have recorded CSAT ratings despite having ticket status set to open or pending.
Mitigation: Captured genuine customer feedback retained in metrics per business practice, but flagged in tables.
Remaining Risk: Mid-journey dissatisfaction may understate ultimate resolution quality.
4. Missing Order ID Linkage (4,107 Tickets / 34.95%) ▾
Issue: 4,107 tickets lack an order_id (general pre-sales, compatibility questions, unverified warranty queries).
Mitigation: Order-dependent analyses (double-dipping, replacement costs) are strictly isolated to validated order records.
Remaining Risk: Cross-channel warranty leakage on unlinked tickets cannot be tracked.
5. Agent Name Collision (Kavya Pandey — A3006 & A3029) ▾
Issue: Two distinct agents share the exact name "Kavya Pandey" (A3006 in Chat Frontline vs A3029 in Logistics).
Mitigation: Enforced strict primary-key integrity across the entire codebase (`agent_id` is the immutable identifier).
Remaining Risk: Zero risk in data/software; operational risk remains if supervisors conduct offline verbal coaching without IDs.
6. Voice IVR Speech-to-Text Transcription Artifacts ▾
Issue: Voice channel interactions contain automated speech-to-text transcripts with phonetic errors and missing speaker tags.
Mitigation: Quantitative engine relies strictly on structured operational metadata rather than unstructured voice transcripts.
Remaining Risk: Qualitative AI triage of voice tickets carries wider error margins than text-native tickets.
7. Category "Other" Miscategorization (1,732 Tickets / 14.74%) ▾
Issue: 1,732 tickets are categorized under generic "Other", obscuring underlying hardware defect and courier delay topics.
Mitigation: Stratified 30 "Other" tickets into the AI audit sample (Stratum 4) to surface latent operational patterns.
Remaining Risk: Full re-categorization of historical tickets requires full-scale NLP processing beyond project scope.
8. Transfer-Field Migration Discrepancy ▾
Issue: Multi-tier handoffs overwrote initial queue attributes during legacy migration.
Mitigation: Tier 2 Escalations agents are evaluated strictly against Tier 2 peers handling multi-day warranty escalations.
Remaining Risk: Precise first-contact resolution (FCR) cannot be computed with 100% certainty for multi-touch tickets.
9. Queue / Case-Mix Selection Effects ▾
Issue: Inherent ticket difficulty varies dramatically across support queues (100% defective units in Tier 2 vs quick chat navigation).
Mitigation: Established 6 operational peer groups; compared agents strictly within their peer cohort.
Remaining Risk: Minor intra-peer group variance (shift timing differences) remains unadjusted.
10. AI Qualitative Uncertainty (30% Exact All-Field Concordance) ▾
Issue: AI classification of unstructured text carries probabilistic uncertainty due to terse agent notes (`cx ok -TF`).
Mitigation: Bounded strictly as an auxiliary diagnostic tool; prohibited from modifying performance metrics, rankings, or shortlists.
Remaining Risk: Supervisors must treat AI tags as decision-support suggestions rather than definitive compliance verdicts.
