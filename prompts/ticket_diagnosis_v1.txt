CLASSIFICATION CRITERIA:
1. primary_root_cause:
   - "agent_handling": Clear evidence of poor communication, dismissive tone, ignoring questions, or process delay caused by the agent.

   - "policy_or_process": Customer frustration is driven by company policy limits (e.g. 7-day DOA window, warranty proof requirements, return pickup delay).
   - "queue_or_case_mix": Delayed response caused by queue backlog, transfer hand-offs, or routing.
   - "customer_or_external": Third-party delivery partner delay, user error, accidental purchase, or unquoted details.
   - "unclear": Insufficient evidence to pinpoint root cause.

2. agent_handling_signal:
   - "none": Agent followed appropriate troubleshooting/procedure; no evidence of handling deficiency.
   - "weak": Minor brevity in notes or slight delay, but within acceptable limits.

   - "appropriate": Action taken directly matched the reported issue under standard policy.

   - "unclear": Notes too brief to evaluate resolution accuracy.

4. customer_sentiment:
   - "negative": Customer expressed overt frustration, anger, or repeated complaints.
   - "neutral": Matter-of-fact inquiry or transactional tone.
   - "positive": Customer expressed satisfaction or gratitude.
   - "unclear": Tone cannot be determined from short transcript.

5. policy_signal:
   - "none": No policy breach or exception detected.
   - "possible": Dual resolution suspected, or Tier 1 approval of warranty replacement without clear RMA.
   - "clear": Documented policy violation (e.g., duplicate refund on replaced order).

6. evidence_quality:
   - "sufficient": Message and notes provide clear diagnostic detail.
   - "limited": Short notes or transcript with partial context.
   - "insufficient": Failed IVR transcript, dropped line, or single-word message/note.

7. confidence:
   - "high": Clear textual proof supporting all classifications.

   - "low": High ambiguity or missing details.
